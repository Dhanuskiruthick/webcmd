# SaaS GrimReaper Terms and Conditions Policy Document

## 1. Terms of Service & Policy Guidelines

### Article I: Scope of Authority & Operational Boundaries
SaaS GrimReaper operates strictly within authorized SaaS organization boundaries using legitimate administrator or workspace owner credentials. It does not perform unauthorized access or bypass security controls.

### Article II: Potential vs Guaranteed Financial Savings
All cost savings estimates generated by SaaS GrimReaper represent **POTENTIAL** monthly and annual license waste calculations based on nominal seat costs. They do not constitute guaranteed vendor billing reductions, which depend on contract terms, minimum seat commitments, and vendor billing cycles.

### Article III: Inactivity Evaluation & Missing Data Policy
1. **Missing Data Protection:** Missing, hidden, unparseable, or future activity timestamps shall never be inferred as inactivity. All records lacking verified timestamps are assigned `NO_DATA` status.
2. **Exempt User Protection:** Administrator-designated exempt users are excluded from offboarding consideration.
3. **Free & Guest License Protection:** Free tier licenses and guest accounts are evaluated as zero direct cost waste.

### Article IV: Mandatory Human Approval & State Machine Rules
1. **Mandatory Approval:** No de-provisioning or offboarding action may execute automatically without explicit human administrator approval.
2. **State Machine Control:** The backend Approval State Machine is the authoritative source of truth. Unapproved candidate seats cannot reach execution layers.

### Article V: Execution Safety & Audit Logging
1. **Post-Verification:** Every execution action must be verified against target platform state post-action.
2. **Sanitized Audit Trail:** All actions, risk classifications, approval state changes, and execution results are logged with automatic credential redaction.
